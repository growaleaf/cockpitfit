What this counts

"Core functions" means the essential bindable functions listed in each module's own official manual — flight controls, weapons release, sensor/radar switching, trim, gear/flaps, and radio — not every keybind DCS exposes. "Slots" means physical buttons, axes, and hats on the controller, counted from the manufacturer's own product page. One physical input covers one function; the tool does not know which functions you personally consider important, only how many exist and how many slots you have.

This tool reports counts and a suggested priority order (flight controls first, then weapons, trim, sensors, gear/flaps, comms). It does not tell you a controller is required or sufficient for any module — that judgment, and any modifier layers or shift states your own controller software supports, is yours.
